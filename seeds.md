# Seeds — details supplied for future entries

Details the reader hands to `/new-entries N …` that no entry has used yet. They are **canon from
the day they're supplied**: an entry may leave one aside, but may not contradict it (the only
licence is the notebook's unreliability about Felix's *own* wants, plans and feelings — see
`CLAUDE.md` §2d). The skill moves a detail to *Used* when an entry gives it real weight, and to
*Retired* if events make it impossible.
